## 📌 Problem Statement

During peak rites in Makkah, extreme temperatures and massive crowd densities lead to heat stroke, disorientation, and medical emergencies. Standard mobile safety applications frequently fail under network congestion, leaving both vulnerable pilgrims and security dispatchers without reliable communication.

---

## 💡 The Solution

**RAFIQ (رفيق)** is an autonomous AI safety agent that connects pilgrims directly to telecom network infrastructure. Powered by **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash** and integrated with **Nokia CAMARA Network APIs (GSMA Open Gateway)**, RAFIQ doesn't just answer questions—it evaluates emergency contexts, reasons through danger, and executes live network operations to preserve life and connectivity.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

* 🤖 **Autonomous Tool Execution:** Gemini 2.5 Flash evaluates distress and triggers network actions without pre-programmed rule chains.
* ⚡ **Nokia 5G Quality on Demand (QoD):** Dynamically provisions elevated 5G network slices for pilgrims in active emergency states to bypass crowd congestion.
* 📍 **Verified Location Mapping:** Uses network-based location verification to pinpoint disoriented pilgrims on GIS maps.
* 📲 **Automated Safety Communications:** Triggers emergency carrier SMS alerts directly to group leaders and emergency services.
* 🚨 **Real-Time Security Command Center:** Auto-synchronizes active incidents to a control-room dashboard with live telemetry and one-click responder dispatch.
